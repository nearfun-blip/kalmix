Introduction

SCOUT PRO GNSS Receiver: Plug-and-Play RTK & Dead Reckoning (DR) Navigation Module with USB-C

Product Overview​

In the world of autonomous driving and robotics, knowing your exact location is everything. The SCOUT PRO takes the complexity out of high-precision GNSS. By combining a high-gain antenna and a powerful receiver into a compact, industrial-grade G-Mouse form factor, we've created the ultimate tool for developers who want to focus on their algorithms, not their wiring.

Why Choose SCOUT PRO?​

  • Plug-and-Play USB-C Connectivity: Say goodbye to jumper wires, soldering irons, and fragile headers. The SCOUT PRO features a native USB Type-C interface with a 3-meter cable, allowing direct connection to your PC, Raspberry Pi, Nvidia Jetson, or industrial controller. It enumerates as a standard serial device, ready for your data stream.

  • Dual-Band L1/L5 Precision: Powered by the MediaTek AG3335 chipset, the SCOUT PRO tracks both L1 and L5 frequency bands. This significantly improves signal reception and multipath mitigation in challenging environments compared to single-band legacy receivers.

  • Built for the Elements (IP67): Robotics don't always operate in a lab. The SCOUT PRO is housed in a fully sealed enclosure rated IP67 waterproof and dustproof. With its robust 3M adhesive backing, you can securely mount it to the roof of a car, a drone, or a mower in seconds.

Choose Your Version​

1. Standard RTK Version​

  • Best for: Open-sky surveying, precision agriculture, and static monitoring stations.

  • Performance: Delivers centimeter-level accuracy with a 1Hz update rate. Ideal for applications where high-speed updates are not critical, but absolute precision is mandatory.

2. RTK + Dead Reckoning (DR) Version​

  • Best for: Urban delivery robots, autonomous driving research, and automated guided vehicles (AGVs).

  • The Problem Solver: What happens when your robot enters a tunnel, a parking garage, or passes under dense tree cover? Standard GPS stops working.

  • The Solution: This version integrates an industrial-grade ST LSM6DSR 6-axis IMU. By fusing GNSS data with inertial measurements (Sensor Fusion), the SCOUT PRO delivers continuous, reliable positioning even when satellite signals are temporarily lost.

  • Performance: High-dynamic output with a 10Hz update rate, ensuring smooth tracking for moving platforms.

Technical Highlights & Integration​

Output Protocol: Standard NMEA 0183 (Default Baud Rate: 115200). Compatible with mainstream navigation software.

Correction Input: Supports RTCM input for Real-Time Kinematics (RTK) operation.

Installation: Industrial-strength 3M VHB adhesive mount for vibration-resistant fixing.

Development Ready: We provide a suite of professional configuration and testing tools (downloadable) to help you get started immediately.

Target Applications​

  • Autonomous Driving Education & Research: Perfect for university labs and ROS-based projects.

  • Robotic Lawnmowers: Reliable boundary handling and path planning.

  • Unmanned Delivery Vehicles: Navigate complex urban environments with the DR version.

  • Precision Agriculture: Automated steering systems for tractors and harvesters.

  • Low-Speed Logistics: AGVs and yard tractors.

  • Drones & UAVs: Ground control station positioning.
