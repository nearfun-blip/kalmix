Dimension

Mechanical Specifications​

This page details the physical dimensions and tolerances of the device and its integrated USB Type-C cable. All measurements are in millimeters (mm) unless otherwise specified.

Product Dimension Overview​

The following diagram illustrates the top, side, and bottom views of the device, including the cable length and housing dimensions. Figure: Mechanical Drawing with Dimensions and Tolerances

Total length: 67.0±0.5mm, Width: 67.0±0.5mm, height: 22.20±0.5mm

Component Details​

1. Integrated Cable​

  • Interface Type: USB Type-C.
  • Total Reach: The cable provides a 3-meter (3000mm) range, suitable for long-distance installations.
  • Flexibility: The Φ 3.0mm diameter ensures a balance between durability and flexibility for cable routing.

2. Main Unit Housing​

  • Form Factor: The device features a compact square design (67mm x 67mm).
  • Mounting Interface: The bottom surface is equipped with Foam Material (highlighted in red in the drawing) for secure mounting and vibration dampening.

3. Material Specifications​

  • Mounting Base: Foam Material.
  • Connector: Standard Type-C plug.
Measurement Notes
  • Tolerance: Dimensional accuracy is critical for installation. Please account for the ± 0.5mm tolerance on the housing when designing mounting brackets or enclosures.
  • Cable Slack: Due to the ± 100mm tolerance on the cable length, it is recommended to allow for slight slack during installation.
Installation

The red area on the bottom indicates the adhesive foam material. Ensure the mounting surface is clean and dry before applying the device to ensure maximum adhesion.
